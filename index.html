Profile

From writing billing code to leading technology.

I started out writing BSS and OSS code for internet service providers, moved to Norway and into architecture, and over twenty years have gone from building systems myself to leading technology across several countries.

One idea runs through all of it. Build a capability once, put it on a shared platform, and reuse it instead of rebuilding it in every product, market or asset. I did that with integration and ERP at Telenor, turned it into a company-wide platform strategy as CTO of Orange Business, and it is now at the core of my work leading technology and architecture at Aker BP.

Along the way I built Statkraft's enterprise architecture function from nothing, and a delivery team of around 120 people in Pakistan that delivered the global ERP Telenor had tried to deliver for years. I live in Oslo and work in Arabic, English and Norwegian.
