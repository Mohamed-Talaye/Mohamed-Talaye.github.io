Short bio

Mohamed Talaye is a technology executive based in Oslo. He leads technology and architecture at Aker BP and was previously Chief Technology Officer of Orange Business. He speaks and writes about platform strategy, AI-native operations, invisible OSS and sovereign cloud.

Long bio

Mohamed Talaye builds technology organisations and the platforms they run on. He leads technology and architecture at Aker BP, where he is building the operating model and the technology platform behind the company's ambition to be the first AI-native oil and gas company.

Before that he was Chief Technology Officer of Orange Business, where he wrote the platform strategy and architecture behind a EUR 150M transformation, the cloud and sovereignty strategies, and drove NetDevOps from idea to a working MVP. Earlier he built Statkraft's enterprise architecture function from nothing, and at Telenor he built a delivery team of around 120 people that delivered the group's global ERP and procurement platforms across eight markets.

He started his career writing BSS and billing code and founded a software company before moving to Norway. He speaks Arabic, English and Norwegian.
