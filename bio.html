Full bio

For press and longer profiles · 137 words

Mohamed Talaye builds technology organisations and the platforms they run on. He leads technology and architecture at Aker BP, where he is building the operating model and the technology platform behind the company's ambition to be the first AI-native oil and gas company.

Before that he was Chief Technology Officer of Orange Business, where he wrote the platform strategy and architecture behind a EUR 150M transformation, the cloud and sovereignty strategies, and drove NetDevOps from idea to a working MVP. Earlier he built Statkraft's enterprise architecture function from nothing, and at Telenor he built a delivery team of around 120 people that delivered the group's global ERP and procurement platforms across eight markets.

He started his career writing BSS and billing code and founded a software company before moving to Norway. He speaks Arabic, English and Norwegian.

Talks I give

Each can run as a keynote of 20 to 40 minutes or as the opening of a panel. I am glad to shape them to your audience.

Talk 1

When OSS becomes invisible

Customers do not want to see the machinery of operations. They want the outcome. This talk shows how OSS moves from tickets and handoffs to an intelligence layer inside the network: intent, closed loops, NetDevOps with AI inside, and customers who co-manage their own services.

Talk 2

From CTO to Chief Visionary Officer

AI agents are taking over more of architecture, coding and delivery. What is left for technology leaders and for developers? A practical look at the AIshore era, why specification writing becomes an engineering skill, and what kind of leader an AI-native company needs.

Read the thinking behind it

Talk 3

Build once, reuse everywhere

Lessons from turning fragmented products, markets and assets into shared platforms at Telenor, Orange Business and in energy. What a platform strategy is, what it costs, where it fails, and how to get business units to pay for something they share.
